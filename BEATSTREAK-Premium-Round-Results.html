THE RULES

Your ten-question set.

This is a manual score sheet for your own music quiz. It does not play music or supply questions.

Consecutive correct answers earn 1, 2, 3… points. A wrong answer earns zero and resets the next answer to 1 point.

Activate your joker before answering to double that answer's points. A wrong answer also consumes the joker. If unused, it activates automatically on question 10.

Premium head-to-head mode gives each contestant a separate ten-question round and one joker. Round 1 is for Player 1; Round 2 is for Player 2. The higher score wins, with equal scores shown as a draw. Back works across both rounds.

Made a mistake? Use Back / correct previous to return to the last question and enter its answer again. Your round is saved locally on this device.

YOUR DATA

A score sheet. That's all.

The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Answers and the score are stored on your device so you can resume your round. In the premium preview, completed solo rounds and head-to-head matches are saved locally with contestant names and completion dates. Nothing is synced between devices.

Clear & reset removes the current round or match, but keeps saved history. Use Delete history in Score history to remove past results. Clearing site data or Android app storage removes all progress and history. Use Export CSV to keep a separate copy.

The Android app works entirely offline and requests no device permissions. When you use the website, your hosting provider may process normal connection information, such as your IP address, to serve the pages.

BEATSTREAK PREMIUM

Try Premium for five completed games or five days from activation, whichever comes first. A game means one solo round or a complete two-contestant match. No automatic charge.

This is a local trial preview. Payments are not connected, and clearing site data can reset this preview. The paid release needs account-based trial enforcement and verified billing.

After the trial, solo scoring stays free. Your saved results remain readable and exportable. New premium games and saving new results require an upgrade. Paid upgrades are not available in this preview.
